1. Can I Apply for a NINO If I Am Not a UK Citizen?

This is the big question that brings you here today: Can I apply for a NINO if I am not a UK citizen?

The answer is a resounding yes. You do not need to be a British citizen to get a National Insurance Number. The UK government does not restrict NINO applications to UK citizens only.

Let us be crystal clear about this: Can non-UK citizens apply for a National Insurance Number? Absolutely. Whether you are an immigrant, an overseas worker, an international student, or a visa holder, you are eligible to apply for a NINO as long as you meet the basic requirements.

The only conditions are:

  1. You must be physically present in the UK – you cannot apply from abroad
  2. You must have the right to work or study in the UK – your immigration status must allow it

There is no requirement to be a UK citizen. There is no minimum period you must have lived in the UK. There is no requirement to be a permanent resident. As long as you are in the UK legally and have the right to work or study, you can apply.

So if you are wondering Can foreigners apply for a NINO in the UK? – the answer is yes. Can non-British citizens get a National Insurance Number? – also yes. Do I need to be a UK citizen to get a National Insurance Number? – definitely not.

Can foreigners get a National Insurance Number in the UK? Yes, the system is open to everyone who is legally in the UK and has the right to work or study. If you need help with your application, the team at NI NO can support you through every step.

2. What Is a National Insurance Number (NINO)?

A National Insurance Number, often called a NINO or simply an NI Number UK, is a unique nine-character reference number that is allocated to every UK National Insurance contributor. It is made up of two letters, six numbers, and a final letter. For example, QQ123456A.

Think of your NINO as your personal tax identification number in the UK. It is used by:

  • HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) to record your National Insurance contributions and income tax
  • The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) for benefits and pensions
  • Your employer to ensure you pay the correct amount of tax
  • The UK government to track your employment history and social security record

Your National Insurance Number stays with you for life. Once you get one, you keep the same number forever. This is why it is so important to apply for one if you are planning to work in the UK.

3. Who Can Apply for a National Insurance Number?

Many people assume that you need to be a UK citizen to get a NINO, but that is simply not true. Who can apply for a National Insurance Number? The answer is: almost anyone who is legally in the UK.

To be eligible, you must be one of the following:

  • Working, about to start work, or actively seeking employment, and have the right to work in the UK
  • Liable to UK Class 1 National Insurance Contributions
  • Making a claim to benefit
  • Entitled to and wish to pay voluntary Class 3 NICs

Here is a quick breakdown of National Insurance Number eligibility for non-UK residents and other categories:

4. National Insurance Number for Different Visa Types

Your visa type determines whether you can work in the UK, and therefore whether you need a National Insurance Number. Here is a detailed breakdown for each visa category.

National Insurance Number for Skilled Worker Visa Holders

If you are coming to the UK on a Skilled Worker Visa, you will need a National Insurance Number to work legally and pay the correct taxes. This is one of the most common visa types for National Insurance Number for visa holders.

Good news: if your visa application was successful, your National Insurance Number may already be printed on your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) card. The Home Office often issues BRP cards with the NINO already included for Skilled Worker Visa holders because they are coming to the UK specifically for full-time employment.

If your NINO is not on your BRP, you will need to apply for one separately through the official process. You can get professional assistance with your application through the NI NO new application service to ensure everything is completed correctly.

National Insurance Number for Student Visa Holders

International students who have a Student Visa UK and are allowed to work part-time during their studies also need a National Insurance Number. This is particularly important for National Insurance Number for international students.

You can only apply for a NINO once you have arrived in the UK and your visa gives you the right to work. However, you can start work before you receive your NINO if you can prove you have the right to work in the UK. Just make sure you apply as soon as possible so you have your number ready for your first payslip.

For detailed guidance tailored to students, check out the National Insurance Number for Students page.

National Insurance Number for EU Citizens After Brexit

Since Brexit, the rules for EU citizens have changed. EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens now need to apply for the EU Settlement Scheme to continue living and working in the UK. This is an important consideration for National Insurance Number for EU citizens after Brexit.

If you have Settled Status or Pre-Settled Status, you are eligible to apply for a National Insurance Number. There is no specified time that you need to live in the UK before you can get a NINO. As long as you have the right to work or study, you can apply.

National Insurance Number for Temporary Workers

If you are in the UK on a temporary work visa, you are still eligible for a National Insurance Number for temporary workers. Temporary workers need a NINO to ensure their tax and National Insurance contributions are correctly recorded.

National Insurance Number for Permanent Residents

If you have Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) or permanent residency status in the UK, you are definitely eligible for a National Insurance Number for permanent residents. In fact, having a NINO is essential for accessing many public services and for employment.

5. How to Apply for a National Insurance Number as a Foreigner

How to apply for a National Insurance Number as a foreigner – here is a step-by-step guide.

Step 1: Check If You Already Have a NINO

Before you start the application, check if you already have a National Insurance Number. Look for it on:

  • Your payslips or P60
  • Your BRP card (if you have one)
  • Your Personal Tax Account
  • Your eVisa account

If you already have a NINO, you do not need to apply again. Use the same number. If you have lost your NINO letter, you can use the NI NO replacement service to request an official HMRC confirmation letter.

Step 2: Gather Your Documents

You will need to provide information about:

  • Your full name and any other names you have used
  • Your date of birth
  • Your nationality and immigration status
  • Your current UK address
  • Your passport or BRP details

Step 3: Apply Online

The official way to apply is through the GOV.UK website. Go to gov.uk/apply-national-insurance-number and complete the online application form.

You will be asked to:

  • Fill in your personal details
  • Provide information about your immigration status
  • Upload copies of your documents if requested

If you need professional help with your application, the team at NI NO offers support with form completion, document checking, and submission to HMRC. They handle everything from translation to document printing and Jobcentre appointments — with support in 70+ languages.

Step 4: Wait for HMRC to Contact You

After you submit your application, HMRC will review it and contact you. They may ask you to:

  • Attend an interview (this is often done over the phone or via video call)
  • Provide additional documents
  • Confirm your identity

Step 5: Receive Your NINO

Once your application is approved, HMRC will send you your National Insurance Number by post. The letter usually arrives within 3 to 4 weeks. However, the whole process can take up to 4 weeks.

Alternative: Apply by Phone

You can also apply by calling the National Insurance Number application line:

  • 0800 141 2079 (England, Scotland, and Wales)
  • 0800 587 0024 (Northern Ireland)

The lines are open Monday to Friday. If you are outside the UK, you can call +44 191 203 7010.

7. Can You Work Without a National Insurance Number?

This is a very common question. The answer is yes, you can start work without your National Insurance Number – but only temporarily.

Here is what you need to know:

  • You can start work as long as you can prove you have the right to work in the UK
  • Your employer will put you on an emergency tax code
  • You will need to provide your NINO to your employer as soon as you receive it
  • If you do not provide your NINO, you may pay too much tax

If you are working in the UK without a NINO, you should apply for one immediately. Your employer needs your NINO to process your payroll correctly and ensure you pay the right amount of tax and National Insurance contributions.

8. National Insurance Number for Immigrants and Overseas Workers

Immigrants to the UK make up a significant portion of NINO applicants. In fact, over 14 million overseas nationals have been issued National Insurance Numbers in the last 20 years.

If you are an immigrant to the UK or an overseas worker, here is what you need to know:

  • You can apply for a NINO as soon as you arrive in the UK (as long as your visa allows work)
  • You do not need to wait for a certain period before applying
  • Your NINO is not linked to your visa status – it stays with you for life even if your visa status changes
  • You can use your NINO even if you leave the UK and come back later

How to get a National Insurance Number as an immigrant is straightforward – just follow the application process outlined above.

National Insurance Number for overseas workers is essential. Your employer will need it to pay you correctly, it ensures you pay the right amount of tax, and it is required for your P60 and P45 forms. It also helps you build a UK financial and employment history.

You can start working before you receive your NINO, but you must apply for one as soon as possible. The team at NI NO can help you with the entire process, from form completion to Jobcentre interview preparation.

9. National Insurance Number for International Students

International students in the UK often work part-time to support their studies. If you are an international student, here is what you need to know about getting a NINO:

  • You can only apply if your Student Visa allows you to work
  • You must apply once you are in the UK
  • You can work up to the number of hours allowed by your visa while you wait for your NINO
  • Your NINO is essential for tax and social security purposes

Many universities provide guidance on how to apply for a National Insurance Number. Check with your university's international student office for support. You can also visit the dedicated NINO for Students page for student-specific guidance on part-time work, placement years, and document requirements.
