1. What Is National Insurance Number Eligibility?

Understanding the National Insurance Number eligibility criteria is the first step before you apply, since not everyone living in the UK automatically qualifies. Broadly speaking, you need to be living in the UK and have the right to work, study or claim certain benefits here.

Once you've confirmed you're eligible, you can move straight on to gathering the documents required for your NINO application and learning how to apply for a National Insurance Number online.

2. Core Eligibility Requirements

To apply for a National Insurance Number, you generally need to meet the following conditions:

  • You are living in the UK at the time of application
  • You have the right to work, study, or claim certain benefits in the UK
  • You are able to prove your identity through accepted documents
  • You are aged 16 or over (younger applicants are handled differently, see below)

You do not need to already have a job to apply, and you do not need to be a British citizen. The system is designed to cover anyone with a lawful basis to be in the UK.

3. Eligibility by Visa and Status

British and Irish Citizens

Most British-born residents are automatically issued a National Insurance Number shortly before their 16th birthday, so an application is only needed if this was missed or the number has been lost.

Skilled Worker and Points-Based Visa Holders

Visa holders sponsored by a UK employer are eligible to apply once their visa is active. See our detailed guide on National Insurance Numbers for Skilled Worker visa holders.

Students

International students with a valid student visa and the right to take up part-time work are generally eligible. Our guide for international students covers this in detail.

Family and Dependant Visas

Dependants on a partner or family visa are usually eligible if their visa grants them the right to work.

You can also refer to the official eligibility guidance on GOV.UK for the most current rules.

5. What If You're Not Sure You're Eligible?

If you're unsure whether you meet the National Insurance Number eligibility criteria, it's worth checking your visa conditions carefully before applying, as an ineligible application can lead to unnecessary delays. ACAS offers free, independent guidance on employment rights in the UK, including questions about right-to-work status.

If you're still not certain, our team at NI NO can help talk through your specific circumstances before you submit anything.

6. Special Cases and Exceptions

  • Under 16s — do not need to apply; a number is usually allocated automatically
  • Returning UK citizens — if you already have a number, you don't need a new one, but may need to recover a lost National Insurance Number
  • Refugees — generally eligible once refugee status is granted
  • Seasonal workers — eligibility depends on the specific visa route

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for a National Insurance Number in the UK? +
Anyone living in the UK with the right to work, study or claim certain benefits is generally eligible to apply for a National Insurance Number.
Do I need a job offer to be eligible? +
No, you do not need a confirmed job offer. You just need the right to work or study in the UK.
Can visitors on a tourist visa apply? +
No, visitors on a standard visitor visa are not usually eligible, since this visa type does not grant the right to work.
What age do you need to be to apply? +
Most applicants are 16 or older. UK residents under 16 do not usually need to apply, as a number is issued automatically.
Are international students eligible? +
Yes, students with a valid visa that permits part-time work are generally eligible to apply.
Do EU citizens need to apply differently? +
EU citizens follow broadly the same process as other non-UK nationals, using their eVisa or settled status details.
Can asylum seekers apply for a National Insurance Number? +
Eligibility depends on individual circumstances and whether permission to work has been granted; it's best to check current guidance.
What if I already have a National Insurance Number? +
You do not need to apply again. If you've lost your number, you should look into recovering it rather than submitting a new application.
Does eligibility depend on nationality? +
Eligibility depends on your immigration status and right to work or study, not specifically on your nationality alone.
