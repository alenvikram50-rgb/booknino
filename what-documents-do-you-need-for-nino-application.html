1. What Documents Do You Need for a NINO Application?

If you are planning to apply for a National Insurance Number, one of the first questions you will ask is what documents do you need for a NINO application. The good news is that the document requirements are straightforward, but you need to make sure you have the right documents before you start your application.

A National Insurance Number (often called an NI Number or NINO) is essential for working in the UK, paying tax, and accessing benefits. To apply for one, you need to prove your identity and your right to work or claim benefits in the UK. HMRC requires specific documents to verify who you are and that you are eligible.

The documents required for NINO application typically include a valid passport, a biometric residence permit (BRP), or other government-issued identity documents. The exact documents you need depend on your personal circumstances — whether you are a UK citizen, an international student, a foreign worker, or from the EU.

In this comprehensive guide, we will explain everything you need to know about National Insurance Number application documents. We will cover what documents are needed, what to do if you do not have certain documents, and how to prepare for a successful application.

2. Documents Required for NINO Application

The documents required for NINO application fall into two main categories:

1. Proof of Identity

You need to prove who you are. This is the most important part of the application. Without valid identity documents, HMRC cannot verify your identity and your application will be delayed or rejected.

2. Proof of Your Right to Work or Claim Benefits

You need to show that you are legally entitled to work or claim benefits in the UK. This is usually proven through your passport, visa, or immigration status documents.

What Documents Are Accepted?

HMRC accepts a range of documents for National Insurance Number application. Here are the main documents you can use:

  • UK Passport — For UK citizens, this is the most straightforward document.
  • Foreign Passport — For non-UK citizens, your passport is essential.
  • Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) — This is often required for non-UK citizens.
  • National Identity Card — For EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens.
  • eVisa — If you have an electronic visa.
  • Home Office Documents — If you are an asylum seeker or refugee.

3. National Insurance Number Application Documents

National Insurance Number application documents are the key to a successful application. Here is a detailed breakdown of what you need:

For UK Citizens

If you are a UK citizen, you will typically need your UK Passport — this is the primary document. If you do not have a passport, you may be able to use other documents, but a passport is strongly recommended.

For EU, EEA, and Swiss Citizens

If you are from the EU, EEA, or Switzerland, you will need your Passport or National Identity Card, proof of your settled or pre-settled status under the EU Settlement Scheme, and your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) if you have one.

For Foreign Workers and Visa Holders

If you are on a visa, you will need your Passport, your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), your eVisa details if applicable, and proof of your visa status.

For International Students

If you are an international student, you will need your Passport, your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), your Student Visa, and proof of your right to work (check your visa conditions).

6. What Documents Are Needed for a National Insurance Number?

What documents are needed for a National Insurance Number? Let us break it down by your situation:

If You Are a UK Citizen

You will need your UK passport (valid or expired — expired passports are sometimes accepted). If you do not have a passport, contact HMRC for guidance.

If You Are an EU, EEA, or Swiss Citizen

You will need your passport or national identity card and proof of your settled or pre-settled status (your status confirmation letter or digital proof).

If You Are on a Skilled Worker Visa

You will need your passport, your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), and your visa documents.

If You Are an International Student

You will need your passport, your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), your student visa, and proof of your right to work (check your visa conditions — usually 20 hours per week during term time).

If You Are an Asylum Seeker or Refugee

You will need your Home Office documents and your identity documents (if available).

7. Proof of Identity for NINO Application

Proof of identity for NINO application is the most critical part of the process. Here is how it works:

Why Identity Verification Is Important

HMRC needs to verify your identity to ensure that you are who you say you are, your National Insurance Number is issued to the correct person, your contributions are properly recorded, and no one else can use your identity to claim benefits or work illegally.

What Counts as Proof of Identity?

Document Acceptable
Valid UK Passport ✅ Yes
Valid Foreign Passport ✅ Yes
Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) ✅ Yes
National Identity Card (EU/EEA) ✅ Yes
Birth Certificate ⚠️ Limited — usually not enough on its own
Driving Licence ⚠️ Limited — usually not enough on its own
Home Office Travel Document ✅ Yes (for refugees)

How to Prove Your Identity

You will need to provide photos of your identity documents during the online application, take a photo of yourself holding your passport (for identity verification), and submit the documents as instructed in the application.

8. Passport for National Insurance Number Application

The passport for National Insurance Number application is the most commonly used document. Here is what you need to know:

UK Passport

If you are a UK citizen, your UK passport is the best document to use. It proves both your identity and your right to live and work in the UK. Valid passports are preferred, but expired passports may sometimes be accepted. Make sure your passport photo is clear and recognisable.

Foreign Passport

If you are not a UK citizen, your foreign passport is essential. You will also need additional documents to prove your right to work. Your passport must be valid, and you will need to show your visa or immigration status.

Passport Photo Requirements

When you apply online, you will need to take a photo of your passport, take a photo of yourself holding your passport, and make sure the photos are clear and in good lighting.

What If You Do Not Have a Passport?

Can I apply for a NINO without a passport? It is possible but more difficult. You may need to provide alternative identity documents, attend an in-person identity interview, or contact HMRC for guidance.

9. National Insurance Number Proof of Identity

National Insurance Number proof of identity is essential for a successful application. Here is a detailed guide:

Accepted Identity Documents

  • UK Passport — UK citizens
  • Foreign Passport — All non-UK citizens
  • Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) — Non-UK citizens with BRP
  • National Identity Card — EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens
  • Home Office Travel Document — Refugees and asylum seekers

What to Do If You Cannot Provide Standard Documents

If you do not have any of the standard documents, you may still be able to apply by contacting HMRC directly, attending an in-person interview at a Jobcentre Plus, or providing alternative documents such as a birth certificate.

Tips for Identity Verification

  • Make sure your documents are valid and not expired
  • Ensure photos are clear and in good lighting
  • Double-check all information before submitting
  • Keep your original documents safe

10. National Insurance Number Application Requirements

National Insurance Number application requirements are straightforward. Here is what you need to meet:

Requirement Details
Age You must be aged 16 or over
Residency You must be living in the UK or planning to live in the UK
Right to Work You must have the legal right to work or claim benefits in the UK
Identity Documents You must be able to prove your identity

You will need to provide a valid passport or other accepted identity document, proof of your right to work in the UK, a photo of yourself holding your passport (for the online application), and photos of your identity documents.

For non-UK citizens, additional requirements include a Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) if you have one, visa documents showing your immigration status, and EU Settlement Scheme status for EU citizens.

11. Can I Apply for a NINO Without a Passport?

Can I apply for a NINO without a passport? Yes, but it is more difficult. Here is what you need to know:

What If You Do Not Have a Passport?

If you do not have a passport, you may still be able to apply using a Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), National Identity Card (for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens), Home Office documents (for refugees and asylum seekers), or Birth Certificate (for UK citizens).

What to Expect

If you do not have a passport, your application will take longer, you may need to attend an in-person identity interview, you may need to provide additional documents, and HMRC may need to verify your identity through other means.

Tips for Applicants Without a Passport

  • Contact HMRC before applying to confirm what documents are needed
  • Provide as many documents as possible to help verify your identity
  • Be prepared to attend an interview if required

13. National Insurance Number Application for Foreign Workers

The National Insurance Number application for foreign workers follows the same basic process but requires specific documents:

Document Required
Valid Passport ✅ Yes
Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) ✅ Yes
Visa Documents ✅ Yes

Tips for foreign workers: Apply as soon as possible after arriving in the UK, check your BRP — your NI Number may already be printed on the back, keep your documents safe, and generate a share code to prove your right to work to your employer.
