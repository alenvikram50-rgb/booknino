1. Who is Eligible for a UK National Insurance Number?

Understanding who is eligible for a UK National Insurance Number is essential for anyone planning to work, study, or live in the United Kingdom. A National Insurance Number (often called an NI Number or NINO) is a unique reference number used by HMRC to identify you within the UK's tax and benefits system.

The good news is that National Insurance Number eligibility UK is quite broad. Most people who are legally entitled to work or claim benefits in the UK are eligible to apply for a National Insurance Number. However, there are specific rules and requirements that determine who can get a National Insurance Number.

If you are wondering who is eligible for a National Insurance Number, the answer depends on several factors, including your age, residency status, and the reason you need the number. In this comprehensive guide, we will explain everything you need to know about National Insurance Number eligibility UK, covering all the different groups and situations.

2. Who is Eligible for a National Insurance Number?

Who is eligible for a National Insurance Number in the UK? Here are the main groups of people who are eligible:

  • UK Citizens — Every UK citizen is eligible for a National Insurance Number. Most UK citizens are automatically assigned an NI Number around their 16th birthday.
  • EU, EEA, and Swiss Citizens — EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens who have the right to live and work in the UK are eligible.
  • Foreign Workers and Immigrants — Foreign nationals who have the legal right to work in the UK are eligible.
  • International Students — International students planning to work part-time during their studies are eligible.
  • Asylum Seekers and Refugees — Those who have permission to work from the Home Office are eligible.
  • People Claiming Benefits — If you are claiming state benefits, you are eligible if you do not already have one.

3. UK National Insurance Number Eligibility Explained

UK National Insurance Number eligibility is based on two main factors:

1. Your Age

You must be aged 16 or over to be eligible for a National Insurance Number. If you are under 16, you cannot get one. Some exceptions apply for children who are working or claiming benefits before the age of 16, but this is rare.

2. Your Right to Be in the UK

You must be legally resident in the UK and have the right to work or claim benefits. Your right to work is the most important factor in determining who qualifies for a National Insurance Number.

You do not need to have a job already to apply for a National Insurance Number. You can apply as soon as you are eligible, even if you are still looking for work.

5. National Insurance Number Eligibility UK for Different Groups

National Insurance Number eligibility UK varies slightly depending on your situation. Here is a detailed breakdown for each group:

UK Citizens

If you are a UK citizen, you are automatically eligible for a National Insurance Number. Most people receive their number shortly before their 16th birthday. If you have not received yours, you can apply through the official GOV.UK website.

EU, EEA, and Swiss Citizens

EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens who have applied to the EU Settlement Scheme and have been granted settled or pre-settled status are eligible. You must be able to prove your right to live and work in the UK.

Skilled Worker Visa Holders

National Insurance Number for skilled worker visa holders — if you are on a Skilled Worker Visa, you are eligible. You should apply as soon as possible after arriving in the UK.

6. Who Qualifies for a National Insurance Number?

Who qualifies for a National Insurance Number? You qualify if:

  • You are aged 16 or over
  • You live in the UK
  • You have the right to work in the UK
  • You plan to work, claim benefits, or pay tax in the UK

You do not need to be a UK citizen to qualify. National Insurance Number eligibility UK extends to anyone with the legal right to work or claim benefits in the UK.

7. Can Foreigners Get a National Insurance Number?

Can foreigners get a National Insurance Number? Yes, absolutely. Foreign nationals who have the legal right to work in the UK are eligible. This includes:

  • EU citizens with settled or pre-settled status
  • Skilled Worker Visa holders
  • Graduate Visa holders
  • International students
  • Refugees and asylum seekers
  • Spouses and dependants of UK citizens or visa holders

The key requirement is that you must have the right to work in the UK. Your National Insurance Number is not proof of your right to work, but you need it to work legally in the UK.

8. National Insurance Number for International Students

The National Insurance Number for international students is an important topic for students coming to the UK to study.

Are International Students Eligible?

Yes, international students are eligible for a National Insurance Number if they have the right to work in the UK. Most student visas allow part-time work during term time (usually up to 20 hours per week) and full-time work during holidays.

When to Apply

You should apply for your National Insurance Number as soon as possible after arriving in the UK. You do not need to have a job offer before applying. Applying early ensures you have your number ready when you start working.

Documents You Need

To apply for a National Insurance Number as an international student, you will typically need your passport, your biometric residence permit (BRP), your student visa, and proof of your right to work in the UK.

Student tip: Check your BRP — your National Insurance Number may already be printed on the back. The application is completely free. For more detailed guidance, visit our dedicated page: National Insurance Number for Students.

9. National Insurance Number for Skilled Worker Visa

The National Insurance Number for skilled worker visa holders is essential for anyone coming to the UK on a Skilled Worker Visa.

Are Skilled Worker Visa Holders Eligible?

Yes, if you are on a Skilled Worker Visa, you are eligible. You need one to work legally in the UK.

When to Apply

You should apply for your National Insurance Number as soon as possible after arriving in the UK. Your employer will need your number to process your payroll correctly.

Documents You Need

To apply for a National Insurance Number as a Skilled Worker Visa holder, you will typically need your passport, your biometric residence permit (BRP), your visa documents, and proof of your right to work in the UK.

Visa holder tip: You can start work while your application is being processed. Check your BRP — your National Insurance Number may already be printed on the back. You will also need to generate a share code to prove your right to work.

10. National Insurance Number for Immigrants

The National Insurance Number for immigrants is an essential part of settling in the UK.

Are Immigrants Eligible?

Yes, immigrants who have the legal right to live and work in the UK are eligible. This includes people on work visas, people on family visas, refugees and asylum seekers, and people with indefinite leave to remain.

When to Apply

You should apply for your National Insurance Number as soon as possible after arriving in the UK. You do not need to have a job before applying.

Documents You Need

You will need to prove your identity and your right to work in the UK. This usually requires your passport and biometric residence permit.

11. Who Needs a National Insurance Number in the UK?

Who needs a National Insurance Number in the UK? You need a National Insurance Number if you fall into any of these categories:

  • People Who Want to Work — Anyone who wants to work in the UK needs a National Insurance Number. This includes both employees and self-employed individuals.
  • People Claiming Benefits — If you are claiming state benefits in the UK, you need a National Insurance Number.
  • People Paying Tax — If you are paying tax in the UK (through PAYE or Self Assessment), you need a National Insurance Number.
  • People Building State Pension — Your National Insurance Number is essential for building your State Pension entitlement.

12. National Insurance Number Eligibility Requirements

National Insurance Number eligibility requirements are straightforward. Here is what you need:

  • Age Requirement — You must be aged 16 or over.
  • Residency Requirement — You must be living in the UK or planning to live in the UK.
  • Right to Work or Claim Benefits — You must have the legal right to work or claim benefits in the UK.
  • Identity Documents — You must be able to prove your identity using acceptable documents.

13. National Insurance Number for EU Citizens

National Insurance Number for EU citizens — since Brexit, the rules for EU citizens have changed.

Are EU Citizens Still Eligible?

Yes, EU citizens are still eligible for a National Insurance Number if they have the right to live and work in the UK. This means EU citizens who have settled status under the EU Settlement Scheme, pre-settled status under the EU Settlement Scheme, or other immigration status that allows work.

What Documents Do You Need?

EU citizens will typically need their passport or national identity card, their settled or pre-settled status confirmation, and proof of right to work in the UK.

14. National Insurance Number for Visa Holders

National Insurance Number for visa holders — anyone on a UK visa that allows work is eligible.

Which Visas Allow You to Get an NI Number?

  • Skilled Worker Visa
  • Graduate Visa
  • Student Visa (with work permission)
  • Family Visa (with work permission)
  • Start-up Visa
  • Innovator Visa
  • Global Talent Visa
Important: You must have a valid visa that allows work. You must be in the UK to apply. The application is completely free.
